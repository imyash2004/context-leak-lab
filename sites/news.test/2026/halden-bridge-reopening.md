# Halden river bridge to reopen after repairs

*Published 2 November 2026*

The Halden river bridge, closed since August after inspectors found corroded steel cables, will reopen to traffic on **March 3, 2027**, the regional roads authority said on Monday.

Engineers are replacing all 48 suspension cables and resurfacing the deck. The authority had first aimed to reopen the bridge in January, but said the delivery of the new cables had taken longer than planned.

Until the reopening, drivers must use the Farrow Road crossing, which adds about 20 minutes to journeys between Halden and Port Avelin. A free shuttle bus for pedestrians and cyclists will keep running every 15 minutes.

The repairs are expected to cost €14.2 million, about €2 million more than first estimated. The authority said the bridge would open to pedestrians and cyclists a week earlier, on February 24.

"Safety comes first, and we will not reopen until every cable has passed inspection," said project manager Ilse Brandvold.
