# Larch County

**Larch County** is a largely rural county in the Corlan region. It covers 1,480 square kilometres of farmland, forest and lakes.

## Government
The county seat is **Pellwick**, where the county council and the district court sit. Before 1956 the county was administered from the smaller market town of Harrow Ford.

## Economy
Agriculture, forestry and food processing are the main industries. A growing number of residents commute to the regional capital, which has pushed up housing costs in the eastern part of the county.

## Geography
The county is crossed by the river Larch, which gives it its name. Lake Ostry, in the north, is a protected wetland and an important stop for migrating birds.
