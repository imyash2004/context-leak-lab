# Oderhaven

**Oderhaven** is a river port town at the mouth of the Oder estuary, with a population of about 18,000.

The town grew around a medieval toll bridge and later became known for brick-making. Its old town has a large number of 17th-century warehouses, several of which are now hotels.

Oderhaven is twinned with [Tessaly Bay](/wiki/Tessaly_Bay) (since 1978) and with Grauwick (since 1991).
