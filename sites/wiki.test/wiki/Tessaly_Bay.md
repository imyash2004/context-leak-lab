# Tessaly Bay

**Tessaly Bay** is a coastal town and former fishing port on the eastern side of the bay of the same name.

## Population
At the 2025 census Tessaly Bay had a population of **12,400**, up from 11,900 at the 2015 census. Growth has come mainly from commuters to the regional capital and from retirees.

## Economy
Fishing declined after the 1980s. Today the largest employers are a ferry operator, a boatyard and the regional hospital. Tourism peaks in July and August, when the town hosts a sailing regatta.

## Twin towns
Tessaly Bay is twinned with **Oderhaven**. The twinning agreement was signed in 1978, and the two towns hold a joint music festival every second summer.

## Landmarks
- [Marrowick Lighthouse](/wiki/Marrowick_Lighthouse), on the headland north of the town.
- The Old Fish Market, now an arts centre.
- St. Brena's church, rebuilt after a fire in 1892.

## Transport
A ferry runs to Marrowick four times a day. The nearest railway station is 9 km inland.
