# Harbour Cup

The **Harbour Cup** is an annual knockout competition for clubs in the Coastal League, first played in 1952.

## 2025 final
The 2025 final was played on 17 May in front of 6,450 spectators. **Saltmarsh United** beat Brightwater Rovers **3–1**, with two goals from forward Ada Merrin. It was Saltmarsh's fourth Harbour Cup title.

## Format
Sixteen clubs enter. Ties are single matches; drawn matches go straight to penalties. The final is hosted by one of the two finalists, chosen by lot.
