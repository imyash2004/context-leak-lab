# Saltmarsh United

**Saltmarsh United** is a football club from Saltmarsh, founded in 1903. It plays at the Mill Lane ground (capacity 5,200) and competes in the Coastal League First Division.

The club has won the [Harbour Cup](/wiki/Harbour_Cup) four times, most recently in 2025. Its fiercest rivals are [Brightwater Rovers](/wiki/Brightwater_Rovers).
