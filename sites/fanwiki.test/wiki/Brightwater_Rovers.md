# Brightwater Rovers

**Brightwater Rovers F.C.** is a football club based in Brightwater, playing in the Coastal League First Division.

## History
The club was founded in **1921** by workers from the Brightwater rope works, and joined the Coastal League in 1934. Its best period came in the 1970s, when it won the league twice (1972, 1976). The club was relegated in 2009 and promoted back in 2014.

## Stadium
The Rovers' home ground is **Kestrel Park**, which they have used since 1928. It holds 6,800 spectators. The main stand was rebuilt in 2017 after storm damage. Before 1928 the club played on a field beside the rope works.

## Colours and nickname
The club plays in navy and white hoops and is nicknamed "the Ropers".

## Honours
- Coastal League champions: 1972, 1976
- Harbour Cup winners: 1966, 1998, 2019
- Harbour Cup runners-up: 2025

## Rivals
The main rivalry is with [Saltmarsh United](/wiki/Saltmarsh_United).
