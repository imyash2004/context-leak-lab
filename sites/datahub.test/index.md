# DataHub.test

DataHub.test is the open data portal of the national statistics office. It publishes annual indicators for every region and county: population, unemployment rate and median rent.

## API
`GET /api/stats?region=<region or county name>` returns all published years of indicators for that area as JSON.

Access requirements are described in the [API documentation](/docs).

## Release calendar
Indicators for 2025 were published on 30 September 2026. Figures for 2026 are due in September 2027.
